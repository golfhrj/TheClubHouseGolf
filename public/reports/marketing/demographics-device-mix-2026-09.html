The 19th hole — the verdict

Golf skews affluent, and affluent skews iOS — so iPhone gets first claim on build priority. But Android still holds roughly 40% of the U.S. market, and Samsung alone accounts for ~55–60% of every U.S. Android phone. Real Android coverage means testing the two Samsung tiers that actually carry that share, at the breakpoints where they diverge.
